    Measured, not promised

    The headline number is the Human Touch Index from our own ledger: zero-touch rate, touches per task, human seconds per merged task, retries, credits and escapes. The first night will look worse than the third. Results quoted on Kiro's frontier-teams page belong to those teams; we cite them as motivation only. Details marked verify in the docs are things not yet confirmed against the live product.
